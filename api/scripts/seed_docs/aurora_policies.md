# Aurora Analytics — Policies

> Fictional policies used as sample corpus for the RAG demo.

## Billing and Refunds

Aurora is billed monthly or annually. Annual plans are discounted by 20 percent compared
to monthly billing. New customers may request a full refund within 30 days of their first
payment. Refunds are not available after the 30-day window, but a plan can be downgraded or
canceled at any time, taking effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Service Level Agreement

The Business plan includes a 99.9 percent uptime SLA measured monthly. If uptime falls
below 99.9 percent in a calendar month, affected customers receive a service credit of 10
percent of that month's fee, and 25 percent if uptime falls below 99.0 percent. Service
credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident. The Starter plan does not include
an SLA.

## Data Retention

Dashboard definitions and connection settings are retained for the life of the account.
Cached query results are retained for 90 days. When an account is canceled, all workspace
data is permanently deleted 30 days after cancellation. Customers can request immediate
deletion by contacting support.

## Support

Starter plan support is handled by email with a target response time of two business days.
Business plan support includes email and chat with a target response time of four business
hours during weekdays. Neither plan includes phone support.

## Security

Aurora encrypts data at rest and in transit. Passwords are hashed. Two-factor authentication
is available on all plans and can be enforced workspace-wide by an Admin on the Business plan.
